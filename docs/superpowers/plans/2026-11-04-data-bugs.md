## Decided (Roshan, 2026-11-04: all three as recommended)

1. **What a frequency change does to the current period.** Recommended: **a schedule's own periods follow its watermark**. The period scan then looks only for entries of the schedules a line had before (turned off and on, or deleted and added again), which is what it was written for. Weekly → monthly on 10 Nov then posts 15 Nov after the weekly 6 Nov, whatever the weekday, and a moved entry no longer hides the next period. *(Review, 2026-11-04: except in a month that starts on a Monday, where the first week's id is the month's; and a change late in a month posts the whole month on top of up to four weeks. See chunk 1's As built.)* The off-and-on results the tests pin stay as they are.
   *Alternative:* keep "a month holding one weekly entry counts as paid" and fix only the moved entry (read an entry's period from its id). November then still depends on the weekday the change was made.
2. **An orphaned schedule in a backup or in the account.** Recommended: **restore and Load accept it.** The app already treats it as a normal state: the poster skips it, and Settings asks for an account ("pick an account"). Refusing it makes one line block the whole file, and the account, when the line is the only thing wrong. The wizard fix (chunk 2) stops the wizard making one; this covers ones already made (by the bug, or by a sync race).
   *Alternative:* fix the wizard only; backups taken in that state stay unrestorable.
3. **Quick add (chunk 3): in or out?** Recommended: **in**, cut to the two realistic cases: a whole number with a leading zero and 3+ digits ("0176" from a phone number) and a comma standing apart from a number ("6 ,50", "6 , 50", typical of dictation). Both are refused, as "6,505" already is: the field says it found no amount, and nothing is saved. Left as they are: "euro2024" ("eur6" is a supported form, and telling a year from a price is a guess), "6€ 50" and "euro 6 50" (the "50" stays visible in the note), "coffee twenty, five" (€25 is likely what was said), "7 40 3 euros" (offers a choice), "lunch for two and cake 12 euros" (already refused).
