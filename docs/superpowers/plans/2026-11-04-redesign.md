# Redesign: warm, outlined, light and dark

**Goal:** replace the default shadcn look with the design Roshan approved on 04-11-2026:
- a warm cream page;
- white cards with a soft 1.5 px outline and a light offset shadow;
- a butter-yellow hero and calm cobalt buttons;
- a colour block for each envelope;
- Bricolage Grotesque headings.

Light is the default; a dark version comes with it, and Settings offers Light, Dark and Same as phone. Dates follow the region (Germany: 04.11.2026).

**Builds on:** `main` as of 04-11-2026: English, German and French are offered and Spanish is complete in dev.

**Decided (Roshan, 2026-11-04):**
1. **The look:** direction B, refined in four rounds of mockups.
   - Round 1: B "Bold" was picked over A Fresh, which Claude had recommended, and C Calm night.
   - Round 2: Roshan found Bold "like a cartoon", so it was softened.
   - Round 3: a middle version was tried and turned down.
   - Round 4: the softened version with Bold's outlines made lighter, version 3's colour block per envelope, and a little more weight. This is the approved look.
2. **Light and dark.** A new phone starts in light; Settings offers Light, Dark and Same as phone.
3. **The ring from C on Home**, beside the "Left to spend this month" number.
4. **One design system, built for the German market,** with a hook for regional layers on top of it later. It follows the global design rules Roshan collected on 04-11-2026: keep the shared component unless a regional need stops people from understanding it or finishing their task, and make that need part of the same system.
5. **Dates follow the region, not a fixed rule.** CLAUDE.md's "DD-MM-YYYY everywhere" rule is dropped; the region decides how a full date looks, so Germany shows 04.11.2026.

**Claude's calls (check while reading):**
- **No existing words change**, with three exceptions:
  - the hero's "over" text becomes one template (see Home);
  - the language hint stops saying dates follow the language;
  - new words for the Appearance section and the date placeholder.

  Roles and accessible names stay, so the specs keep their selectors, and every icon is `aria-hidden`.
- **A full date looks the same in every language.** With the region Germany, English, French and Spanish readers also see 04.11.2026, the form on the German documents a student meets. CLDR's combined locales (`en-DE` gives 04/11/2026) would tie the format to the language again. Short labels such as "Mon 26 Oct" stay in the reader's language.
- **Money and numbers stay with the language for now** (English keeps €6.50). Moving them to the region is a follow-up.
- **German is the reference language:** every screen is designed and reviewed in German first, then English and French.
- **The ring fills with what has been spent**, like the envelope bars, so a full ring and a full bar mean the same thing. It is `aria-hidden`, since the amount is already in the text.
- **Category blocks are tinted from the existing `--cat-*` colours** with `color-mix`, so dark mode follows for free.
- **The theme picker is a vertical radio group.** A three-way segmented control can't hold "Comme le téléphone" in a third of 375 px.
- **No new dependencies.** lucide-react and next/font are already in.
- **`quick-add-field.tsx` waits for the mic button.** Another session is adding a mic button there. Until that PR merges, quick add takes the new look only through `Input` and `Button`. Chunk 12 then changes only its props and classes.
- **Screens are shown, not committed.** `test-results/` is git-ignored and `docs/` is notes only, so Claude shows each chunk's before-and-after screens to Roshan in the session, and the PR body says which screens changed.

### Shapes and components

- **Flexible text boxes:** nothing that holds text gets a fixed height. Buttons, chips, tabs and nav items use a minimum height and let a long label wrap (`whitespace-normal`, balanced), rather than shadcn's `h-8` with `whitespace-nowrap`. No text is set inside images.
- **Cards:** card fill, the 1.5 px outline, an 18 px radius and the offset shadow. A `Card` primitive goes in `src/components/ui/card.tsx` (shadcn's base-nova card, restyled), with a `tone` prop for a category block and an `accent` variant for the hero.
- **Buttons:** at least 44 px tall (50 px for main actions).
  - `default` is cobalt with the outline and the offset shadow. On press the shadow shrinks to 1 px and the button moves 2 px; nothing moves under reduced motion.
  - `outline` is card-coloured with the outline; `ghost` and `link` stay quiet.
  - A new `chip` variant is an outlined pill, filled with the foreground colour when pressed. It's for the Tips chips and, in chunk 12, quick add.
- **Fields:** `Input`, `NativeSelect` and `DateField` are 50 px tall, with the 1.5 px outline and a 14 px radius. Focus adds the cobalt ring.
- **Tabs:** a segmented control. A muted, outlined track holds the segments; the active one is white with the outline.
- **Bars:** 10 px tall, rounded, filled in the category colour on the track, with no outline. The numbers are always in text (the visible line and `aria-valuetext`), so a bar is never the only way to read them.
- **Tags:** category tags are tinted pills with the outline and the category icon, not tilted. Badges are round outlined stickers.

### Themes

- **Stored per phone, like the language:** localStorage `studentsbudget-theme` holds `light`, `dark` or `system`, and is `light` when nothing is stored. It is not synced and not backed up.
- **No flash:** `themeHeadScript()`, an inline ES5 script next to `languageHeadScript`, runs before the first paint. It sets `class="dark"` and `color-scheme` on `<html>` and updates the theme-color meta. If storage can't be read, the page stays light.
- **Staying in sync:** `ThemeAttributes`, built like `LanguageAttributes`, follows a choice made in another tab (the storage event). Under Same as phone it also follows the phone's own changes (`matchMedia`).
- **Dark styles already work:** `@custom-variant dark (&:is(.dark *))` stays, and `chart.tsx`'s `.dark` themes and `chart-parts.tsx`'s pairs work as they are.
- **Settings:** an Appearance section goes right after Language: a radio group with Light, Dark and Same as phone.
  - Each row is 52 px, outlined, with an icon (sun, moon, phone) beside its label; the chosen row is the accent fill with the offset shadow.

  - In German the section is "Aussehen", with Hell, Dunkel and Wie das Handy. It can't be "Darstellung": the charts already use that word for their style switches, and a hidden page stays mounted, so two controls with the same name would trip the specs' strict mode.

### One design system, regional layers on top

The base design is the German-market design. Following Roshan's rules, this is what stays global and what may adapt:

| Stays global (the base) | May adapt, and what drives it |
|---|---|
| Brand colours, type hierarchy, spacing, radius, components, outlines and shadows | Fonts for other scripts: the **language** |
| The bottom nav and its five places, core interactions, feedback, accessibility | Reading direction (RTL): the **language** (the registry's `dir`) |
| What the app can do, and the shape of each workflow | Words and help: the **language** (dictionaries, city content) |
| | Dates: the **region** (in this plan); numbers and currency: the language for now (a follow-up) |
| | Region-specific content or imagery: the **region layer** |

- **The region registry** (`src/i18n/regions.ts`, next to `languages.ts`): one region, `de`, which is the default. Each entry holds the region's own name ("Deutschland"), its date pattern (`dd.MM.yyyy`), its date-and-time pattern (`dd.MM.yyyy, HH:mm`) and the order and separator the date field's placeholder uses. As with `languages.ts`, nothing else hard-codes a region.
- **Dates by region:**
  - `formatDate(d, region = DEFAULT_REGION)` and `formatDateTime` in `src/lib/dates.ts` read the region's pattern, so the 16 call sites stay as they are. Stored values stay `IsoDate` strings: only the display changes.
  - The language hint in Settings says today "Money and dates follow the language", which will no longer be true. It becomes "Money follows the language; dates follow the region (Germany)" in all four languages.
  - The date field's placeholder is built from the region's order and the language's letters: TT.MM.JJJJ in German, DD.MM.YYYY in English, JJ.MM.AAAA in French, DD.MM.AAAA in Spanish.
  - The e2e specs read expected dates through the app's `formatDate`, not as literal strings, so a later region doesn't mean another 38 edits.
  - The MCP server's instructions (`create-server.ts`, `prompts.ts` and five tool notes) say to write dates as DD.MM.YYYY, and so does CLAUDE.md's dates rule.
  - There is no region picker yet: like the language picker, it would show nothing while only one region can be picked.
- **The region layer, built here as a hook:**
  - The root layout sets `data-region="de"` on `<html>`. With one region it is static; a second region would pick it the way the head script picks the language.
  - `src/app/regions/de.css` is an empty layer with a header comment, imported after the base tokens.
  - `regions.test.ts` allows a region file to set only `--region-*` tokens, never colours, fonts, spacing, radius or components. That keeps a region from becoming a fork of the design.
  - CLAUDE.md gets an "Adding a region's design layer" recipe. By Roshan's own rule the layer stays nearly empty: the test makes "keep the shared component" the default.
- **Writing systems:**
  - The language registry gains a `script` field (`Latn` for all four languages today).
  - Fonts follow the language, not the region. A non-Latin language gets its font through `next/font` in the layout and a `:lang(<code>)` block in `globals.css` that sets `--font-sans` and `--font-heading`.
  - A test fails when a registry language's script is neither `Latn` nor given such a `:lang()` block.
- **Right to left:**
  - Restyled code uses logical classes (`ps-`, `pe-`, `ms-`, `me-`, `start-`, `end-`, `text-start`), and directional icons such as chevrons and arrows flip under `rtl:`. Logos, the ring and the shadow's offset don't flip, because they aren't directional.
  - A unit test refuses physical `left`/`right` classes in `src/**/*.tsx`, with an allow-list that shrinks as files are restyled. `quick-add-field.tsx` stays on the list until chunk 12.
- **Already in place:** language names in their own language ("Deutsch", "Français"), no flags, icons always beside a label, and status never shown by colour alone (overspending is said in words; the active nav item also has `aria-current`).
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Germany's BFSG (the European Accessibility Act, from 28-06-2025) points to EN 301 549, which asks for WCAG 2.1 AA. The secondary sources found tie its scope to e-commerce and similar consumer services and exempt microenterprises that provide services, so it likely doesn't bind this app; 2.2 AA covers it either way. Nobody has checked the statute itself.
